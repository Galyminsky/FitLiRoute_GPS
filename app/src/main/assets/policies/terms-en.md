## 2. Purpose of the App

FITLiROUTE GPS is a free mobile application designed to record, display, and save travel routes using GPS and other available device location technologies.

The App provides the following core features:

- determining the user's current location;
- recording traveled routes;
- displaying routes on a map;
- calculating distance, duration, and speed;
- saving route history locally on the device;
- viewing and deleting saved routes;
- configuring available recording and display settings.

Certain features require location permissions, an internet connection, and access to relevant Android system services.

## 3. GPS and Location Services

The App uses device location information to record travel routes.

Recording is initiated by the user through the START button after the necessary permissions have been granted.

While recording is active, the App may continue accessing location information when minimized or when the device screen is locked through an Android Foreground Service.

Users are responsible for starting and stopping route recordings appropriately.

GPS accuracy and recording continuity depend on the device, satellite reception, environmental conditions, Android settings, and power management restrictions.

The developer does not guarantee absolute accuracy of coordinates, speed, distance, or time measurements.
